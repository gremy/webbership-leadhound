Checkpoint 1

LeadHound console board

Static mockups of the redesigned console, one stylesheet (board.css) that becomes console.css. All data is invented.

How to look at it

Every page follows your computer's light or dark setting. To see the other theme, change the OS setting or add data-theme="dark" (or "light") to the html element in the browser's inspector. Check each page at 1280 px and 390 px wide.

Screens

11 screens · the shell (header, switcher, theme, gear, sub-nav) is on every page

  • Dashboard

    After sign-in. Widgets across both products; Status summary worst first, each line links to its Status section.

  • Status

    Mailboxes, Server and jobs, Reoon, Claude (remaining credit is an estimate), Jev. Every section shows its last update; grey means not checked yet.

  • Leads home

    Today at a glance, room per mailbox as meters, next actions, starred leads and a real empty state.

  • Leads list

    Filters, star toggles, bulk select with the Send now (3) action bar.

  • Lead page

    Profile, findings, the grounded draft preview. Send now is primary; Star and Skip are secondary.

  • Batch page

    Drafts with send toggles (one skipped), Approve and Approve and send now.

  • Send confirm

    Warnings you can go past (amber, with numbers and codes), hard stops (red, no override), a lead waiting on its email check, the same-domain and 25-per-run rules.

  • Jobs home

    Today's matches, tracked (empty state), runs and rules.

  • Settings

    Under the gear. Tabs: Sending window, Switches, Mailboxes, Campaign settings, Claude top-up, Account.

  • Gear menu

    The gear opened (a details element, works without JS): Settings, Controls, Mailboxes, Account, Sign out.

  • CRM ideas

    Proposal only: the eight CRM ideas with a small sketch each. Pick which to build.
